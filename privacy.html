Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

TaskTracker ("the app") is a personal desktop application for macOS. It is built and used by a single person, its developer. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used and where it is stored.

Data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app asks for these permissions:

  • Gmail (read-only): message subjects, senders, short snippets and dates, so emails that need action can be listed and flagged.
  • Google Drive (read-only): the names and contents of documents the owner uses for study planning.
  • Google Sheets (read-only): spreadsheets the owner uses for planning.
  • Email address: to show which Google account is connected.

The app never sends, deletes or changes emails or files.

How the data is used

Google data is used only to show the owner their own information inside the app, for example a list of emails that need action or deadlines from planning documents. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to train AI models.

Where the data is stored

  • Data the app reads is saved in a local database on the owner's computer. There is no TaskTracker server and nothing is uploaded to one.
  • Google sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer.
  • The app runs a local interface on the same computer (127.0.0.1). It cannot be reached from the internet and needs a secret key. The owner's own personal assistant software uses it to answer the owner's questions about their schedule and email.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with, sold or transferred to anyone. The only exception is the owner's own tools on the same computer, as described above. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

The owner can disconnect a Google account in the app's Settings at any time. This deletes the stored sign-in tokens. Access can also be removed at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data folder removes all locally stored data.

Children's privacy

The app is not offered to the public and does not collect data from anyone other than its owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised by opening an issue at github.com/WKoehl/tasktracker-site.
