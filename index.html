A personal organiser for the IB Diploma

TaskTracker is a private desktop app for macOS, built by one student for their own use. It brings school assignments, deadlines, email, reminders and grades together in one place.

What it does

  • Shows school assignments and upcoming deadlines in one list
  • Flags emails that need action, from the owner's own Gmail accounts
  • Reads the owner's own Google Drive documents and Sheets used for study planning
  • Tracks IB grades, predicted grades, CAS/DofE hours and university applications

How it uses Google data

TaskTracker asks for read-only access to Gmail, Google Drive and Google Sheets. That data is only used to show the owner their own information inside the app, and it is stored only on the owner's computer. Read the privacy policy for details.

TaskTracker is not offered to the public. Only its developer uses it.
